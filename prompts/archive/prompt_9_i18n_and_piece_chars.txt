In folder "assets/i18n", I have files for language
specific strings and characters used in chess notation.
I plan to use them later. 

For now, I just want you to fill out missing languages.
We start with dictionary "piece-chars". The idea is, 
that we use the dict values later when printing out
chess notation. The value denote the characters used
when printing out for example "queen on square a5", 
which would be "Qa5" in English, but "Da5" in German.

I started with "en.typ" and "de.typ". Please complete
the other language files.
