1. Move generator:
Yes, we need a fully "legal" move generator in the end
but it may sensible to split the generator into
- a pseudo-legal generator, that doesn't test for "king-in-check?"
    yet and just puts a move in the internal move list.
- a part afterwards, that checks whether the king (of the side
    for which we just generating legal moves) would be in check.

As for the programming language: I strongly prefer a pure Typst
solution here. I don't think, Typst is too slow. In a possible
"tournament" PGN file with many games, we would just look at the
roster for the game result and ignore the actual move text. For a
single game, the performance of Typst is plenty.
